How these numbers are calculated

Each reading measures energy (kWh) over 15 minutes. Four readings make one hour. The heatmap and daily pattern average only hours with all four distinct quarter-hour readings; missing readings are never treated as zero. Monthly totals include all valid recorded readings, including partial days. Daily averages use only complete 96-interval days. Peak demand is the largest interval’s kWh × 4, an average kW over those 15 minutes, not an instantaneous peak.

Times follow the local clock in your report. Weeks start on Sunday. Duplicate intervals keep the latest revision; equal-revision ties keep the last imported row. This CSV does not identify repeated daylight-saving clock hours, so those intervals cannot be unambiguously distinguished from duplicates. DST days may be marked incomplete or undercounted. Solar/Battery Exports are energy sent to the grid from solar panels or batteries, not total solar production or battery discharge. Consumption is energy drawn from the grid, not necessarily your home’s total use.

Imported readings are saved in local storage on this browser and restored when you return. Clear data removes the saved copy. Demo data is not saved. Browser settings or private browsing may limit persistence. No uploads, analytics, external fonts, or remote chart libraries.

Plan comparison assumptions

Estimates use calendar months and the selected meter’s grid consumption, including estimated readings. Free-energy windows use the report’s local clock. Daily windows include the start time and exclude the exact end time. Weekly windows run continuously from the selected start day/hour through the entire selected ending hour (for example, Friday 7:00pm through Sunday 11:59:59pm). Utility delivery is charged on all consumption. Each qualifying credit applies once per month; multiple qualifying credits add together. Credits use all consumption, including free-energy hours. Bills have a $0 floor, with no credit carryover.

Energy, delivery, base charges, and individual credits are rounded to cents before calculating the monthly bill. Taxes, other fees, solar/battery export credits, and actual utility billing-cycle dates are not included. Use the rates and terms from each plan’s Electricity Facts Label; no current provider rates are supplied. Plan definitions are saved on this browser separately from usage. Clear data removes readings; use Remove on a plan to delete it.

START WITH YOUR METER

Get your Smart Meter Texas data

  1. Sign in or register.

    Visit SmartMeterTexas.com ↗. Have your electricity bill handy for your ESI ID, meter number, and retail electricity provider when registering.

  2. Choose your interval report.

    On the dashboard or View Energy Data page, choose “Energy Data 15 Min Interval” as the report type.

  3. Select your dates.

    Choose roughly the last year, then select “Submit Update”. If the portal limits the range, download smaller ranges and import all the CSVs together.

  4. Export and bring it here.

    Select “Export My Report” for CSV. The Green Button option produces XML, which this app does not accept. Save the CSV, return here, and choose your files.

An internet connection is needed to get your report. Once this app is downloaded, analysis works offline.
